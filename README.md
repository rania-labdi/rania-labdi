I’m an **Embedded Systems Engineer and researcher** interested in developing intelligent robotic systems by combining **embedded computing, artificial intelligence, computer vision, and robotics**.

I’m particularly interested in bringing AI algorithms from research into **real-world robotic platforms**, where perception, decision-making, and control must operate under computational and hardware constraints.
